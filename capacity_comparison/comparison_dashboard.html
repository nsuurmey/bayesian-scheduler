A. Timeline View (Gantt-Style)

Each project shows five horizontal bars — one per method — illustrating when the method thinks the project starts and how long it runs. Faded extensions indicate uncertainty ranges (P10–P90 for PERT, 90% credible interval for Bayesian). Compare how the optimiser (RCPSP) may shift projects later to respect resource caps, while PERT simply stacks them at their earliest dates.

B. Monthly Capacity Heatmap

How heavily is the team loaded each month, according to each method? Cells are coloured by average utilisation across all resource types (data scientists, geoscientists, PMs). Green means comfortable headroom; yellow is tight; red means the method predicts over-capacity. Hover for per-role breakdowns.

C. Risk & Uncertainty Comparison

Each method quantifies uncertainty differently. PERT produces Monte Carlo duration distributions (box plots). Bayesian shows posterior-predictive distributions that incorporate historical data (violin plots). Scenario Planning shows utilisation bands (P10–P90) across three scenarios, with the dashed line marking 100% capacity.

D. Summary Scorecard

A quick-reference table for leadership. Total duration is the sum of expected project weeks (not calendar time). Peak utilisation is the highest single-resource month. "Months Over Cap" counts months where at least one resource type exceeds 100%.
